Binding

Binding is a collection of three essays about beauty, ugliness and the judgments we make about design. Each essay asks the same question from a different place: who decides what is beautiful, and what happens when we disagree?

Yoon Soo Lee writes about growing up with judgment and learning to practice love. Alexandra Lange writes an open letter arguing that criticism is a form of love. Steven Heller questions the cult of ugly design coming out of the graduate schools of the early 1990s.

  2. Criticism = Loveby Alexandra Lange
